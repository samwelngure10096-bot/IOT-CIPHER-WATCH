// HOW THIS WORKS
  1. Register a device below — its real IP is probed with actual ICMP ping / TCP connect on a background timer, not simulated.
  2. Go to Passwords and generate one for that device.
  3. If it has a management connector (e.g. SSH), clicking Apply rotates it automatically over the network. Otherwise, paste it into the device's own app/admin page, then click Apply to record it.
  4. Remove a device permanently with the ✕ on its card — it's deleted from the backend's encrypted store, not just hidden.
